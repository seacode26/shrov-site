Momentum, reversal, moving averages, RSI, Bollinger position, drawdown depth, close location, volatility and skew are all chained together at |r| ≥ 0.5. Only negative_skew and rev_1d stand alone in both universes.

Some pairs are near-identities that the family labels concealed. mom_1m against rev_21d is −0.97 — they are the same number with opposite signs, filed under different families. lowvol_60 against downside_dev is +0.97. The same pairs top the matrix in both universes, which is what a structural result looks like rather than a fitted one.

3.3The framework is confirmed; the inputs are empty

Equal-weighting N independent signals should multiply IC by √N_eff. Using the a-priori signs — not the signs that worked, which would be fitting 31 binary parameters to the same decade the answer is read from — it does.

Combining lifts the IC by roughly what the eigenvalues predict — not by √31, and not by nothing. The breadth machinery works as advertised. It amplified a negative number.

A note on precision: the large-cap agreement (2.22 against 2.36) is closer than this statistic deserves. Its denominator is a mean IC near −0.005, close enough to zero that the ratio is unstable, and the small-cap replication gives 3.71 against 2.49. The directional claim survives; the apparent 6% accuracy was luck.
