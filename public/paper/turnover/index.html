2.6 · TurnoverCosts stop mattering; the signal was never there

Twelve configurations per universe (lookback 60/120/250 × hold 21/63/126/252). Turnover falls from 22–30× to 0.6–3.6×, and in small caps the spread drag collapses from 43 points to under 2. The binding constraint is removed.

What it reveals is a random field. Excess over equal-weight ranges from −228 to +225 points with a standard deviation of ~129, and the largest swing from a single parameter step is 302 points. A real effect produces a gradient; this does not. The standout cell (+225%, or +4.82%/yr) was chosen from twelve draws whose expected maximum under a pure-noise null sits about 210 points above the mean — the observed 225 is almost exactly what noise looks like once sorted.

2.8 · Market-neutralThe construction worked; the argument did not

Every result so far carried the market factor: one giant correlated bet whose variance swamps everything else. The stated rationale was explicit and falsifiable — at a 4%/yr excess, 16% volatility gives IR 0.25 and needs 64 years to reach t = 2, while 6% volatility gives IR 0.67 and needs 9. Same alpha; removing variance nobody is paying for was supposed to be the highest-leverage change available.

Beta collapsed from 0.96 to roughly zero — the construction does what it says. The variance reduction did not follow. Volatility stayed near 20% instead of falling toward 6%, because the short leg cancels the market factor while contributing its own idiosyncratic risk: you replace one correlated bet with forty individual ones. The arithmetic that justified the whole exercise does not survive its own test.

The alpha is also negative — shorting the bottom of a momentum ranking through a decade-long bull market loses money — and three of five books hit margin calls.

One constraint is worth recording because it makes this permanently a measurement rather than a strategy: short selling is not available in an IRA, the natural home for an account like this. The long/short book exists to answer "is there alpha here at all" with the statistical power to see it, not to be deployed.
