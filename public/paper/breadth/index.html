§ 3

The central result: 31 signals are 6 bets

What survived the neutral construction is the other half of Grinold's fundamental law. IR = IC × √breadth pays the square root only if the bets are independent. Thirty-one signals that all say "buy what went up" are one bet with thirty spare copies.

We built 31 signals across six declared families — momentum, reversal, volatility, liquidity, technical, shape — from the published anomaly literature. Each states the prior fixing its sign before seeing data, because choosing signs afterwards is 31 free parameters and would fit anything. Two pairs are deliberate near-duplicates, planted as positive controls for the correlation matrix.

Controls before conclusions

The instrument was tested against a signal that is the forward return (must measure IC ≈ 1) and against pure noise (must measure IC ≈ 0 and effective breadth ≈ the signal count). An instrument that cannot see a perfect edge cannot be trusted when it reports none, and one that finds structure in noise would flatter every result in this paper.
